From the Desk

Curiously crafted costumes & climactic characters.

Miss Texas 1988 is a Seattle drag artist, performer, host, writer, and all-around creative oddball. She dabbles in everything from the silly to the sublime — curiously crafted costumes, climactic characters, and a sense of humor that's done its homework.

Catch her as a cast member of Tush, The Sunday Sip, Lashes, Glory Hole, and Jawbreaker, and as host of High F@ggotry. You can also find her rampaging around as a reporter for The Stranger, and on OUTtv as the winner of Camp Wannakiki Season 5.

Off-stage, she's finishing a BFA in Graphic Design at Seattle Central Creative Academy — class of 2026 — with editorial, identity, and product design pieces in the portfolio.

With love from Capitol Hill,
Nico
