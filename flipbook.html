While Texas Bops around in front of the camera as a reporter for The Stranger's social media, Nico covers local art as a regular columnist.

Through my recurring column "Spotlit In Seattle" I've had the honor of interviewing local celebs like Dina Martina and covering the successes of our home town champions like RuPaul's Drag Race star Jane Don't. I've highlight the city's extreme talent from dance, to puppetry, visual art, music and more.
